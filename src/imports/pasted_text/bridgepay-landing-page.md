Design a modern, premium, responsive landing page for a fintech web application called "BridgePay".

## About BridgePay

BridgePay is a Hire Purchase Marketplace that connects customers with verified stores. Customers can browse stores, explore products, apply for hire purchase, pay an initial deposit, receive their product after approval, and continue paying in installments until the product is fully owned.

The design should communicate trust, affordability, simplicity, and professionalism. It should feel like a premium fintech platform similar in quality to Stripe, Shopify, Airbnb, Apple, Revolut, and Klarna.

The design must use Auto Layout, reusable components, proper spacing, and be fully responsive for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.
